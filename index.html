从课程编排到实时伴学，从插件生态到 AI 内容生成，覆盖学习的每一个环节。 From course orchestration to real-time companionship, from plugin ecosystem to AI content generation, covering every aspect of the learning journey.

M1

千人千面的智能课程编排引擎Personalized Course Orchestration

输入用户的学习目标、当前知识水平、可用时间、偏好语言等，AI 检索全网优质学习资源，基于知识图谱和教学法原则，自动编排为体系化、精确到每日任务的个性化课程计划。 Input learning goals, knowledge level, available time, and language preferences. AI retrieves quality resources and orchestrates them into systematic, day-by-day personalized course plans based on knowledge graphs and pedagogical principles.

输出类 Coursera 体验的完整课程 — 每一课包含教学视频、讲义/幻灯片、课后小测、笔记模板、作业。系统自动动态重排后续课程日程。 Output: Coursera-style complete courses — each lesson includes videos, notes/slides, quizzes, note templates, and assignments. The system automatically reschedules subsequent lessons.

M2

实时 AI Copilot 伴学Real-time AI Copilot

用户在观看视频课程时，可随时暂停并在右侧 Copilot 交互区提问。关键技术差异：得益于底层对视频内容的深度语义理解，用户无需补充任何上下文，可直接引用时间戳提问。 Users can pause anytime and ask questions in the Copilot panel. Key differentiation: thanks to deep semantic understanding of video content, users can reference timestamps directly without providing additional context.

延伸能力：AI 基于对整套课程及用户个人学习画像的理解，主动识别薄弱知识点，并针对性生成强化练习、笔记和小测。 Extended capability: AI identifies weak knowledge points based on understanding of the entire course and user learning profile, and generates targeted reinforcement exercises, notes, and quizzes.

M3

AI-Native 插件市场AI-Native Plugin Marketplace

Copilot 不只是一个 ChatBot — 它是一个可扩展的教学工具平台。英语词汇场景调用"单词听写"插件、数学场景调用"交互式坐标系画板"、文科场景调用古诗/课文默写插件。 Copilot is not just a ChatBot — it's an extensible teaching tool platform. English vocabulary triggers dictation plugins, math scenarios trigger interactive coordinate canvases, humanities trigger poetry/text dictation.

开放生态（规划中）：后续开放 Plugin 开发规范，允许第三方开发者和教育创作者共建插件市场 — 形成"内容生态 + 工具生态"双飞轮。 Open ecosystem (planned): Plugin development specs will be released, allowing third-party developers and creators to co-build the marketplace — forming a "content + tool ecosystem" dual flywheel.

M4

完整的 AI-Native 生态Complete AI-Native Ecosystem

得益于 AI 对整套课程及用户个人画像的理解，AI 不仅可以找到最适合用户的学习课程和路径，还可以针对用户的痛点、薄弱点、知识重点、易忘点进行针对性的内容生成。 With AI's understanding of the entire curriculum and user profile, it not only finds optimal learning paths but also generates targeted content addressing pain points, weak areas, key concepts, and easily forgotten knowledge.

例如用户对知识点 A 掌握薄弱，在观看课程时多次提问并倒退进度条反复观看，AI 可针对该知识点生成具有针对性的小测、笔记和作业。 For example, if a user struggles with concept A, asking multiple questions and rewinding repeatedly, AI generates targeted quizzes, notes, and assignments for that specific concept.

普通 AI 用同一条流水线服务所有人；我们为每一个学习者建立一份会进化的认知模型——你的学习基因组。每一次学习都在更新它，每一次教学决策都在优化它。 Generic AI serves everyone with one pipeline. We build an evolving cognitive model for every learner — your learning genome. Every study session updates it; every teaching decision optimizes it.

BKT · IRT

知识状态 = 概率Knowledge as probability

"会了"不是 yes/no，而是一个 0.93 的掌握概率。每一次作答都在更新你对每个知识点的真实把握。"Mastered" isn't yes/no — it's a 0.93 probability. Every answer updates your true grasp of each concept.

FSRS

遗忘动力学建模Forgetting dynamics

现代间隔复习算法（Anki 官方同款），在你"该忘又没忘"的黄金点精准推送复习，记忆效率最大化。Modern spaced-repetition (same engine as Anki) pushes review at the golden point — just before you'd forget — for maximal retention.

KST

认知结构推断Knowledge space

知识缺口是一张可推导的图：卡在 B，往往是因为缺了 A。系统据此补的是根因，不是表象。Gaps form a derivable graph: stuck at B usually means missing A. We fix the root cause, not the symptom.

ACTIVE LEARNING

期望学习速率最大化Optimal teaching

不按固定顺序灌，而是选"最能让你进步的下一个知识点"——把你的时间花在边际收益最高的地方。Not a fixed syllabus, but the next concept that maximizes your progress — your time goes where marginal gain is highest.

META-LEARNING

教学策略自进化Teaching evolves

教法本身被它自己的结果训练：你听例子学得快，以后就多给例子。教学规则靠证据确认或证伪。The method trains on its own results: learn fast from examples, get more examples. Rules are confirmed or refuted by evidence.

DESIRABLE DIFFICULTIES

刻意安排困难Desirable difficulties

检索、间隔、交错组合拳——故意安排"该忘又没忘"的时刻，让记忆长得更牢，而非更轻松。Retrieval + spacing + interleaving — we engineer desirable difficulties so memory grows stronger, not easier.
